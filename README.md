# Mythbreed Derby

Breed, raise and race horses and creatures of legend.

**Play:** https://bizket02.github.io/mythbreed-derby/

## Put it on your phone

- **iPhone / iPad:** open the link in **Safari**, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the link in **Chrome**, tap the **⋮** menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

It opens full screen from its own icon, keeps your stable on the device, and works offline once it has loaded.
New versions download in the background and start the next time you open it.

**Moving your stable:** in the game, open Settings → *Move your stable* → Export, then Import in the other copy.
